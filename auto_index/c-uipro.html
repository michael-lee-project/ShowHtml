Coverage

Every refrigerant era

> Refrigerant generations changed the parts. We tool for all three, so the vehicle on your lift is never too old or too new.

R-12
1980s - 1993

Classic systems. Hard-to-find hoses and driers for vintage vehicles, still in our catalog.

R-134a
1994 - 2012

The volume era. Barrier hoses, evaporators and condensers for the largest parc on the road.

R-1234yf
2013 - today

Modern low-GWP systems. Correct fittings and materials, not interchangeable with R-134a.
